Alarms · AL1–AL4

Edit a time then hit Set, or flip On/Off. The GBD‑200 has 4 alarms — AL1 uses feature 0x15; AL2–AL4 share the 0x16 block.

Settings · 0x13

24-Hour Time12h / 24h format
Button Toneoperation beep
Auto Lighttilt to illuminate
Key Vibrationhaptic feedback
Keep Auto Light Onre-enable while connected (beats the 6h auto-off)
Sleep Scheduledisconnect overnight to spare the battery

Each toggle reads the watch's current 0x13 block, flips one bit, and writes it back — all other settings preserved.

Keep Auto Light On is a companion-side workaround: the GBD‑200's firmware turns Auto Light off by itself after about 6 hours and there's no setting to stop that. While connected, Grid Console re-enables Auto Light whenever the watch lets it lapse.

Sleep Schedule protects the watch's multi-year battery: during these hours Grid Console releases the Bluetooth link so the watch isn't kept awake, then reconnects when the window ends (while the app is open). Set it to your sleeping hours — you won't be tilting for the light then anyway.

Names: up to 14 chars, A–Z 0–9 and / + - _ ! ? &. Each slot is mm:ss (minutes 0–60, seconds 0–59). Empty = skipped.

Watch Condition is a fixed status signature the watch reports — shown as Normal while it’s responding (the raw bytes are listed under it for reference). The GBD‑200 has no battery gauge, over Bluetooth or on the watch; it only flashes a low‑battery warning when the coin cell is nearly dead, so there’s no level or percentage to show.

Daily Step Goal · 0x43

Sets the watch's daily step target (feature 0x43) — the watch's own step-progress display uses it. Saved on this phone too, so your goal shows even before the watch syncs. 1,000-step steps, 1,000–50,000.

Profile · 0x45

Wristleft / right
Gendermale / female

Toggle = right wrist / female (the official app's profile bits). Height, weight and birthday are stored too but not yet decoded reliably, so they're left out to avoid writing bad values.

Console

What this is: the GBD‑200's own LCD is fixed in ROM and can't be reskinned. This is a companion console that reads and writes the watch's real settings over Bluetooth — time, alarms, timers, settings, notifications and world time, all verified on hardware. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Casio.
