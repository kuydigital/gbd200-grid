GRID//CONSOLE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Grid Console (“the app”) is a Bluetooth companion for Casio GBD‑200 (module 3506) watches. We designed it to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to communicate directly with your watch, to read and change its settings (time, alarms, timers, world time, display options) and to read the step/calorie totals the watch has logged. This communication happens locally between your iPhone and your watch. The data is shown to you in the app and is not transmitted anywhere else.

Network

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts’ content delivery network (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). As with any web request, this can expose your device’s IP address to Google under Google’s privacy policy. The app makes no other network requests and sends no personal data over the network.

Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose utility, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project’s page at github.com/kuydigital/gbd200-grid.

Grid Console is an independent companion app and is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Casio. “Casio”, “G‑SHOCK” and the watch model names are trademarks of their respective owners.
