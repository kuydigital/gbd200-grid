GRID//CONSOLE — Help & FAQ

Companion app for Casio GBD‑200 (module 3506) watches.

Which watches are supported?

Any Casio watch built on module 3506 — the GBD‑200 series (GBD‑200‑1, ‑2, ‑9, ‑7, ‑1A1, RD‑4, LM‑1, UU‑1/UU‑9, SM‑1A5/1A6, and regional editions). Check the caseback or manual for “module 3506”.

How do I connect the first time?

  1. On the watch, hold the upper‑left button for about 2 seconds, select PAIRING, then CONNECT — pressing the upper‑right button to confirm each.
  2. In GridConsole, tap Connect and allow Bluetooth.
  3. Accept the iOS “Bluetooth Pairing Request” when it appears.

After that one-time pairing the watch stays bonded, and the app reconnects on its own.

It won’t connect, keeps dropping, or a read fails — what do I do?

Forget the watch and pair again. Open iOS Settings → Bluetooth, tap the ⓘ next to CASIO GBD‑200, choose Forget This Device, then reconnect from GridConsole and accept the pairing prompt. This clears a stale bond and resolves most connection or read problems (including activity/steps not loading).

Also make sure no other app (such as Casio’s own) is holding the watch at the same time — Bluetooth allows one connection.

Do I need the official Casio app?

No. GridConsole pairs and talks to the watch on its own.

Will keeping it connected drain the watch battery?

The watch is built for multi‑year battery life. GridConsole keeps radio traffic minimal, and Keep Auto Light On includes a Sleep Schedule that releases the Bluetooth link during the hours you set (e.g. overnight), so the watch isn’t kept awake.

My watch’s Auto Light keeps switching itself off.

That’s the GBD‑200 firmware disabling tilt‑to‑light after about 6 hours. Turn on Keep Auto Light On (Set tab) and GridConsole re‑enables it whenever it lapses.

Does the app collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
